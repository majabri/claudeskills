## Regulatory Scope

| Jurisdiction     | Key Law          | Applies When                              |
|------------------|------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| EU               | GDPR             | Any EU resident's data is processed       |
| UK               | UK GDPR          | Any UK resident's data is processed       |
| California       | CCPA/CPRA        | >$25M revenue OR 100K+ consumers          |
| Canada           | PIPEDA / Law 25  | Commercial activity involving Canadian data|
| UAE              | Federal PDPL     | UAE residents' data                       |
| Saudi Arabia     | PDPL             | Saudi residents' data                     |
| 20+ US States    | Various          | Check consumer volume thresholds          |

## Key Principles
1. Accountability — document everything
2. Data Minimization — collect only what you need
3. Transparency — tell users what you do with their data
4. User Rights — honor access, deletion, correction, portability
5. Lawful Basis — every processing activity needs a legal reason
6. Privacy by Design — build privacy in from the start
7. Consent Quality — freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous
