# PM L1 — Plan & Coordinate

Project planning tier. Altitude: a single project from initiation through close.

## What belongs at L1

- Project charter, business case, mandate
- Stakeholder register, interest/influence map, communications plan
- WBS, scope statement, in/out-of-scope list, acceptance criteria
- Milestone plan, Gantt, release plan, sprint roadmap
- Risk register, RAID log, risk response planning
- RACI matrix, team structure, capacity planning
- Budget baseline, cost estimate, burn rate
- Backlog: epic/feature/story decomposition, MoSCoW/WSJF/RICE prioritization
- Project closure: lessons learned, benefits realization, sign-off

## Project Charter Sections

1. Project title and sponsor
2. Business objective (why this project?)
3. Scope summary (in and out of scope)
4. Key stakeholders and roles
5. High-level milestones and target dates
6. Budget envelope
7. Key risks and constraints
8. Success criteria
9. PM authority level

## RACI
- **R** = Responsible (does the work)
- **A** = Accountable (owns outcome; one per row only)
- **C** = Consulted (input before decisions)
- **I** = Informed (notified of decisions)

## Risk Register Columns
Risk ID | Description | Category | Likelihood | Impact | Exposure | Owner | Response | Status

## Stakeholder Map
High influence + High interest → Manage closely
High influence + Low interest → Keep satisfied
Low influence + High interest → Keep informed
Low influence + Low interest → Monitor

| RICE | Product teams |

## Questions to ask before generating any artifact

- What is the project trying to achieve?
- Who is the sponsor? Key stakeholders?
- Hard deadline or target date?
- Known constraints (budget, headcount, tech)?
- Greenfield or existing system?
- What methodology is the team using?
