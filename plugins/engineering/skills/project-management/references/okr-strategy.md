# OKR & Strategic Alignment Reference

## When to Load This
Load for OKRs, connecting projects to strategy, quarterly business reviews, portfolio prioritization by alignment, or outcome-based delivery.

## Core Formula
"I will [Objective] as measured by [Key Results]."

## Anatomy of an OKR

**Objective**: Qualitative, inspiring, time-bound (quarterly). "Dramatically improve customer onboarding experience."

**Key Results**: Quantitative, measurable, 3–5 per objective, graded 0.0–1.0 (target: 0.7 = stretch).
- ✅ "Time-to-first-value reduced from 14 days to 5 days"
- ❌ "Complete onboarding redesign" (output, not outcome)
- ❌ "Hold 3 workshops" (activity, not result)

## OKR Levels
Company OKRs (CEO, annual+quarterly) → Team OKRs (quarterly) → Individual OKRs (optional)

## OKR Cadences
Quarterly: set + grade | Weekly: check-in (confidence + blockers) | Monthly: mid-quarter review | Annual: strategic review

## Connecting OKRs to Projects
Company OKR → Team OKR → Initiative (project) → Sprint Goal → Stories

Every active project traces to ≥1 Key Result. If it doesn't → OKR is missing or project shouldn't exist.

## Portfolio Prioritization by OKR
Score projects: (OKR impact × confidence) ÷ effort. Name what's being deferred and why.

## OKR Grading
0.0–0.3: little progress | 0.4–0.6: meaningful but short | 0.7–0.9: strong stretch achieved | 1.0: fully achieved (was it ambitious enough?)

## Common Mistakes
| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Output KRs ("launch X") | Ask "so what?" — the answer is the real KR |
| Too many OKRs | 3–5 objectives; 3–5 KRs each |
| OKRs = performance review | Separate grading from evaluation |
| Top-down cascading | Bidirectional: top sets direction; teams negotiate contribution |
| OKR theater | OKRs visible in every sprint planning and prioritization meeting |

## OKRs in Agile
Portfolio OKRs → SAFe Strategic Themes | Program OKRs → PI Objectives | Team OKRs → Sprint Goals
