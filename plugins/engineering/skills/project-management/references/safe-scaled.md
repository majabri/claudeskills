# SAFe & Scaled Agile Reference

## When to Load This
Load for SAFe, LeSS, Scrum@Scale, PI Planning, Agile Release Trains, enterprise agile, or coordinating 5+ agile teams.

## SAFe 6.0

### Four Configurations
| Config | Scale | When |
|---|---|---|
| Essential SAFe | 1 ART; 50–125 people | Most common starting point |
| Large Solution | Multiple ARTs | Complex multi-ART systems |
| Portfolio SAFe | Strategic portfolio | Investment decisions needed |
| Full SAFe | All of the above | Large enterprise |

### Agile Release Train (ART)
5–12 agile teams (5–11 people each). Fixed Program Increments (PI): 8–12 weeks. Synchronized sprint cadence.

**ART Roles:** Release Train Engineer (RTE), Product Manager, System Architect, Business Owners

### PI Planning (2-day event)
Day 1: Business context, architecture briefing, team breakouts drafting sprint plans
Day 2: Finalize PI Objectives, ROAM risks, confidence vote (Fist of Five ≥3), planning retro

Output: sprint plans for 8–12 weeks, ART PI Objectives (committed + stretch), risk ROAM log, dependency map

### Backlog Hierarchy
Epic (Portfolio) → Feature (Program) → Story (Team)

### WSJF Prioritization
(Business Value + Time Criticality + Risk Reduction) ÷ Job Size
Higher score = higher priority. Accounts for cost of delay.

## LeSS
One PO, one backlog, 2–8 self-managing teams. Minimal roles/ceremonies. Best for: strong Scrum culture, willing to restructure around products.

## Scrum@Scale
Networks of Scrum teams. Scrum of Scrums for cross-team coordination. MetaScrum for product decisions. Less prescriptive than SAFe.

## Choosing a Scaling Framework
| | SAFe | LeSS | Scrum@Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prescription | High | Low | Medium |
| Enterprise integration | Strong | Weak | Weak |
| Team autonomy | Medium | High | High |
| Best for | Large enterprise + PMO | Product-focused org | Scrum-first teams |

## Common Failure Modes
- SAFe feels like waterfall → PI Planning was treated as fixed-scope commitment
- Teams don't coordinate mid-PI → weekly dependency sync needed
- Scaling before mastering Scrum → pilot 1–2 teams first
